# Framed Googly Eyes: client verification

Run these checks on Minecraft 1.21.8 with Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge. The shared `FramedEyeGameTestsLogic` and each loader's `FramedEyeGameTests` cover simulation and eligibility; these checks cover the rendering integration and client lifecycle.

Use Googly Eye items, including dyed eyes and eyes modified with glowstone. For easy observation, temporarily set the server's `ambientBehaviorPool` to `["blink", "side_eye", "stare"]` and shorten its ambient interval, then restore the original settings. Framed eyes use the mean configured interval as their slot length, extended to the longest eligible expression when necessary to prevent overlap. Loose eyes exclude cross-eye; a head layout with paired eyes can use its configured cross-targets.

1. **Mounting and gravity.** Place regular and glow frames on walls facing each horizontal direction, on a floor, and on a ceiling. Insert eyes and cycle through every item rotation. Irises face out of the frame, wall-mounted pupils settle toward world down, and upward/downward-facing eyes have no consistent in-plane gravity. Move the camera around: gravity must not follow the camera. Turning an eye changes its resting direction in item coordinates.
2. **Expressions and independence.** Fill neighboring frames with copies of one eye. They blink, stare, and glance independently. One eye never crosses toward another frame. Expressions finish and release their overlays. Set an interval shorter than the longest expression and confirm clean, nonoverlapping playback.
3. **Appearance and lighting.** Compare white, dyed, and glowing eyes in regular and glow frames, in daylight and darkness. Loose framed-eye diameter uses the renderer's 3x multiplier, while thickness, item colors, and vanilla frame lighting remain intact; a glowing eye retains its emissive rendering during expressions.
4. **Invisible frames.** Make an occupied frame invisible with vanilla entity data editing. Its eye keeps animating without visible wood or backing. Check wall, floor, and ceiling placements.
5. **Contents and removal.** Remove an eye, insert another with different colors, replace it with a Slimy Eye or diamond, empty the frame, and break the frame. No old pupil or expression appears on an unrelated item. Reinserting an eye uses its current appearance.
6. **Render-context isolation.** While several framed eyes express, inspect the same item in inventory, on the ground, and in third person. GUI views animate independently; ground views retain centered pupils; third-person hands animate independently. A first-person held eye keeps its own movement, hurt, heal, and ambient behavior. Eyed head items animate in frames. The same head items animate independently in GUI slots and keep centered eyes on the ground and in first-person hands; third-person hands animate independently; worn heads show none. Placed heads retain their existing animation.
7. **Visibility and resource reload.** Look away long enough for tracker eviction, then return. The current expression is reconstructed rather than replaying the missed history. Reload client resources and confirm framed and held eyes still render.
8. **Pause and world changes.** Pause a single-player world during an expression; simulation freezes and resumes with the world. Leave/rejoin, reload the world, and travel between dimensions. No former world's framed-eye state leaks into the next world. Multiplayer menus do not pause a running server.
9. **Settings.** With ambient behaviors disabled or an empty pool, neither loose eyes nor heads play expressions; pupil physics remains active. With ambient enabled and a cross-eye-only pool, paired heads can cross-eye but loose eyes remain expressionless. Restore a blink-only pool and confirm both resume blinking. Disable the server master switch or client `disableGooglyEyes`: loose framed-eye items remain visible with centered, static pupils; head eyes obey their existing display gates. Reenable the switch and confirm animation resumes. Apply server configuration changes using the loader's normal reload/restart mechanism.
10. **Two viewers.** Have two players observe the same frame, with one arriving during an expression and after unloading/reloading the area. Expression type, phase, and seeded choices agree approximately in time. Physical pupil trajectories may differ between clients.

11. **Eyed head items.** Frame each eligible vanilla head type with stored eyes, then rotate it through every item orientation on walls, floors, and ceilings. Check configured eye positions, colors/glow, gravity, blinking, and paired cross-eye. Head models and their attached eyes retain their normal configured size; only loose eye items receive the diameter multiplier. This release supports vanilla head mappings; optional mod heads without mappings remain unsupported. Replace a head with another species, another variant/appearance, a plain head, and a loose eye. No prior layout survives the change. Two viewers, including one arriving mid-blink, see the same expression and participating pupils.

Frame animation adds no custom packets or saved fields. Existing frame items retain their colors and glow through normal saving, drops, and replacement.
