# Inventory eye animation: client verification

Run these checks on Minecraft 26.1.2 with Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge. `GuiItemEyeGameTestsLogic` covers presentation ownership, replacement, UI time, eligibility, and head layouts. GUI hook execution, icon transforms, clipping, and atlas behavior require a client.

Use loose Googly Eyes with different iris/cornea colors and glow settings, and eligible head items that already carry eyes. Temporarily shorten the synced ambient interval and select blink, side-eye, stare, and cross-eye to make expressions easy to observe; restore the original settings afterward.

1. **Views and independence.** Put identical copies in several hotbar, player-inventory, chest, and creative-menu slots. Each presentation has independent animation. Loose eyes never cross-eye; paired heads use their configured partners. Plain heads, Slimy Eyes, and unrelated items retain their ordinary rendering.
2. **Moving contents.** Move, split, merge, and shift-click stacks. Drag a carried eye or head across occupied slots. Count changes do not continuously restart an icon, while species, variant, and appearance changes show the replacement immediately. The moving cursor item retains its own expression.
3. **Size and overlays.** Animated loose icons keep their existing inventory size. Head models keep their normal GUI transforms and configured eye placements. Check stack counts, durability bars where applicable, enchantment foil, tooltips, hover highlights, and selected-hotbar effects. Blink/grow expressions remain within vanilla's icon clipping policy; the frame-only diameter multiplier does not enlarge inventory icons.
4. **GUI orientation.** Observe eyes while turning the player and camera, changing the world view behind an inventory screen, and resizing the window or changing GUI scale. Pupil down follows the icon's screen pose, not the camera's world orientation. Logical slot and cursor identities survive layout changes; generic previews may restart when relocated.
5. **Pause and settings.** On a GUI presentation visible in a pausing screen, confirm expressions continue while the world is paused. Ambient-off or an empty pool stops expressions but keeps pupil physics. A cross-eye-only pool animates paired heads but not lone eyes. Master/client disable makes loose icons static and preserves the existing head display gates. Apply settings using the loader's normal reload/restart mechanism.
6. **Caches and lifecycle.** In newer versions, identical items in separate slots must animate independently without frozen or exchanged images. Scroll creative entries out of view and back, close/change containers, reload resources, disconnect/rejoin, and change worlds. No previous view's layout or colors leak into the next. Confirm that ordinary items still render normally.
7. **Head appearances.** Test all eligible vanilla head types and, on NeoForge with Heads 4.0.0 installed, Heads-mod heads. Check a woolly sheep head in inventory, frames, and hands: it must show a single set of eyes despite its separate face and wool models. Verify eye-bearing items retain colors/glow and paired behavior. Their inventory views animate separately from the same head items in frames and from placed heads.
8. **Frame and hand regression.** Follow `framed-eyes-testing.md`: framed loose eyes retain their larger diameter, head models keep normal size, and world animation pauses normally. First-person held eyes retain movement, hurt, and heal reactions. Third-person hands animate independently; ground views retain resting pupils. No GUI expression leaks into a world draw.

GUI animation uses local view identities and an independent UI tick clock. It adds no item identifiers, components, saved fields, or packets.
