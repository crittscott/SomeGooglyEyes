## Heads, skulls, and boats

- Give placed skeleton, wither skeleton, zombie, creeper, piglin, and player heads googly eyes with a Slimy Eye. Remove them with Optometrist shears.
- Eyed heads keep their eyes, colors, and glow when broken and placed again. Heads dropped by eyed mobs can inherit their eyes.
- On Forge, supported heads from the Heads mod and Twilight Forest's trophies and skull candles can have eyes too. Adding a candle to an eyed skull preserves its eyes.
- Give vanilla boats, chest boats, and bamboo rafts eyes with a Slimy Eye, and remove them with Optometrist shears. Boat eyes react to movement and play idle expressions. They survive world saves; breaking and replacing a boat resets them.
- Googly Eyes and eyed head items now animate in item frames, inventory slots, and third-person hands. Loose eyes held in first and third person share their hurt and heal reactions.
